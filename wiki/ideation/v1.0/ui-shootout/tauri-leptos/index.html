Stack 1 — Tauri 2 + Leptos

A pure-Rust front-to-back desktop stack: Tauri 2 provides the OS WebView shell, native menus, system tray, sidecar process management, and signed/notarized installers; Leptos renders the UI as Rust compiled to WebAssembly, with fine-grained reactivity and the ability to share serde types with mcpd directly. This page is the summary and table of contents for the Tauri+Leptos evaluation lane of the shootout.

Bottom line

A pure-Rust front-to-back stack that shares types and logic with mcpd via a common crate, ships a small system-WebView binary, and delivers genuinely native a11y on macOS and Linux — at the cost of a slower-than-JS dev loop, an officially-unsupported SSR setup[?] (Tauri tells you to use Leptos in SSG/CSR mode), and a fragmented WebKit surface on Linux.

  • Best when: the team is Rust-first, wants shared types between mcpd and the GUI, values footprint over instant-HMR turnaround, and is willing to invest in macOS notarization.
  • Worst when: the team needs sub-second hot-reload, wants a deep frontend hiring pool, or expects SSE/streaming to "just work" identically on WKWebView and WebKitGTK without per-platform testing.
