stoic-badger-sandbox

Stream 2 of 5 — leveraging Nix and devenv.sh for per-MCP-server isolation, with runtime sandboxing layered on top (bubblewrap, sandbox-exec, AppContainer) and a deliberate escape hatch for users who can't or won't adopt Nix. The stream surveys reproducibility, runtime isolation primitives, container alternatives, secret management, and lands on a two-tier recommendation: declarative Nix profiles as the primary path, OCI containers as the supported fallback.
